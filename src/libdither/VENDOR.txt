Exception (local patches in color_models.c, kept minimal and upstreamable):
- distance_lab2000: hp_ave (radians) converted to degrees via RAD2DEG before
  T / delta_theta (was used as degrees directly). Upstream issue
  https://github.com/robertkist/libdither/issues/10. Verified against the
  Sharma/Wu/Dalal 2005 reference dataset (33 pairs, kL=kC=kH=1): before the
  fix 23/33 pairs failed (max err 6.63), after the fix 33/33 pass (max err
  < 1e-4). Hue sweep L=50/C=40 showed up to 29% relative error before.
- distance_ccir: removed /255 normalization (divisors 255000->1000, channel
  diffs unscaled) because FloatColor channels are 0..1, not 0..255. The old
  form suppressed the luminance term ~255x vs the chroma term and flipped
  palette-choice ordering vs the author's intent (demonstrated by exhaustive
  coarse-grid comparison: fixed 0..1 ordering == original byte-scale ordering).
